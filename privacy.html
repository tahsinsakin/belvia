Privacy policy

Effective 13 September 2026. Publisher: Tahsin Sakin, Ankara, Türkiye. Contact: tahcem17@gmail.com.

BudVia is an on-device trip list. It does not create accounts. It does not run a BudVia server. It does not include analytics, ads, crash upload, or social login.

What stays on the phone

If you type a trip, that text stays on that device in browser storage under the key budvia-v2. Older copies may still sit under belvia-v2. BudVia reads both, then writes only budvia-v2. BudVia does not send that text to Tahsin Sakin.

What leaves the phone when you tap

  • A ticket row can open Wizz, FlixBus, Airbnb, Booking, or that company’s public site.
  • Map can load OpenStreetMap tiles and can open Apple Maps or Google Maps.
  • LinkedIn opens LinkedIn.

Those services are not BudVia. Their rules apply after they open.

Cookies and tracking

BudVia does not set marketing cookies and does not run analytics. See the cookie page. No cookie banner is shown because there is nothing extra to accept.

Children

BudVia is not aimed at children. It does not collect personal data on a server from anyone.

Delete

Use Clear this phone in the app, or clear site data for tahsinsakin.github.io, or uninstall the app.
